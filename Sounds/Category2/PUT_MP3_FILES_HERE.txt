Place your sound files for Category 2 in this folder.
Supported formats: .ogg, .mp3, .wav

Example:
  Sounds\Category2\nice.ogg

Then add the exact name (WITHOUT the extension) to the [2] list in Sounds.lua:
  [2] = {
      "nice",
  },

A full WoW client restart is required before new files show up - see README.md.
