Place your sound files for Category 1 in this folder.
Supported formats: .ogg, .mp3, .wav

Example:
  Sounds\Category1\lets go.ogg

Then add the exact name (WITHOUT the extension) to the [1] list in Sounds.lua:
  [1] = {
      "lets go",
  },

A full WoW client restart is required before new files show up - see README.md.
